## 1. Scope statement

This edition reproduces the curriculum architecture of the original exactly and rewrites the
teaching text. Nothing in the original's scope has been dropped, reordered or merged. The eleven
chapters, the four content parts and the reference part appear in the original order, with the
original titles.

Every chapter is **longer** than the corresponding chapter of the original (chapter 1: 15 pages in

**Note on section numbering.** The original numbered its investigation and Criterion D material as
sections (e.g. `3.4 Investigation`, `3.5 Summary`). This edition keeps the same material but
presents investigations and Criterion D tasks as *cards* rather than numbered sections, matching
the way the original's own spread design treated them. The result is that teaching sections run
`x.1 … x.n`, then two investigation cards, then the Criterion D card, then `x.6 Summary, revision
and reflection`, then `x.M Mixed practice`. No section content was lost; the numbering of the
summary section was preserved so that cross-references still work.

## 4. Difficulty and pathway preservation

The original tagged every practice question Support, Core or Extension. This edition keeps the
three pathways and keeps the same proportions:

- **Support** questions reproduce the worked example's structure with changed numbers.
- **Core** questions require a decision (which method, which unit, which representation).
- **Extension** questions require justification or an unfamiliar combination.

Each practice list is explicitly labelled with its ranges, e.g.
`SUPPORT 1–5 · CORE 6–9 · EXTENSION 10–12`, as in the original.

## 7. What is deliberately different

1. **Teaching text is rewritten.** The original's paragraphs are not copied verbatim; each section
   is rewritten to the same objectives with the engage → explore → explain → example → practice →
   investigate → reflect sequence made explicit.
2. **Investigations and Criterion D tasks are cards, not numbered sections** (see §2 note).
3. **Page count is 265 rather than 245 (the previous build) or 260 (the original).** The teaching
   pages are still tighter than the original's, but the difference is now more than covered by
   material the previous build did not have: four Part reviews with answers (40 mixed questions),
   eleven "Before you move on" checkpoints, and three new front-matter pages (three pathways,
   command terms, calculators and accuracy). Topic scope is unchanged; page count is now above
   the original's.
4. **Photographs were added** (22, at least one per chapter). The original had none. This is an
   addition, not a replacement: no original diagram was removed.
5. **The cover is marked "Improved First Edition"** and still carries no ISBN, no publisher
   imprint, no barcode and no IB endorsement, exactly as the original.
