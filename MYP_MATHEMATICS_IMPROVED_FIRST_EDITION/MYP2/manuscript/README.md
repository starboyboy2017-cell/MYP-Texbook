## What the extracts contain

All running text, definitions, key ideas, common errors, worked-example steps, questions,
captions, table cells, answers, glossary and index entries. Chapter files are written from the
same content modules the book is typeset from, so the extract and the printed page cannot drift
apart. Front and back matter are read back from the delivered PDF, because those pages are drawn
directly rather than assembled from blocks.

## What the extracts do not contain

* **Letter-spaced display labels** (part name, chapter number, stage kicker, panel kicker,
  running head, folio). They belong to the page design, not to the reading text, and would
  extract as one letter per space.
* **Vector artwork.** Figures and diagrams are drawn into the PDF and have no text layer; their
  captions are present, so every figure is still identifiable, and the figure is described in
  the surrounding prose.
* **Typesetting detail.** Line breaks in the extract are the line breaks of the typeset page,
  so paragraphs are wrapped, not reflowed; hyphenation at line ends has been rejoined.

The PDF in `../final/` is the authoritative edition. These files exist so that the text can be
searched, counted and quoted without opening the PDF.
