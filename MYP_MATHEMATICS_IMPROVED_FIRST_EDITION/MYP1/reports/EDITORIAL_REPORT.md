# Editorial report — MYP Mathematics 1, Improved First Edition

Author of the edition: **SADIKOU MOUKODDIM**
Scope: **MYP Year 1 only.** Years 2–5 of the source series are out of scope and are not
part of this edition.

---

## 1. What this edition is

A complete, self-contained Year 1 student coursebook, written and typeset from scratch,
that keeps the visual identity of the source first edition while rebuilding the
mathematics, the pedagogy and the page system. It is a new book, not an annotated copy:
no sentence, exercise, diagram or page layout is taken from the source.

## 2. Structure

Five parts, each opening with a full-bleed part plate:

1. **Number, structure and proof** — Ch 1 number systems and place value;
   Ch 2 powers, expanded form and order of operations; Ch 3 factors, multiples and primes.
2. **Fractions, decimals and percentages** — Ch 4 fractions and decimals; Ch 5 percentages
   and applications.
3. **Algebra as compressed reasoning** — Ch 6 patterns, expressions and equations.
4. **Describing space and belonging** — Ch 7 lines, angles, triangles and constructions;
   Ch 8 sets and classification; Ch 9 perimeter, area, surface area and volume.
5. **Reading the story in data** — Ch 10 data, tables and graphs.

Every chapter runs the same seven-stage rhythm, and the stage labels are printed on the
page so a lesson can be planned around a stage rather than a page number:

| Stage | What the student does | Where it sits |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | MYP unit planner: statement of inquiry, concepts, global context, inquiry questions, criterion/strand map, ATL skills, prerequisite check, inquiry pathway | chapter opener spread |
| 02 | Learning intentions and success criteria ("You are learning to / You have succeeded when you can") | planner pages |
| 03 | **Engage** — a situation, photograph or puzzle that makes the mathematics necessary | first content page |
| 04 | **Explore** — a conjecture is formed before any rule is given | before the first section |
| 05 | **Explain** — definitions, notation, properties, worked examples, common errors | sections *n*.1–*n*.6 |
| 06 | **Practice**, **Investigate and apply**, criterion-mapped mini-task | after the last section |
| 07 | **Reflect and consolidate** — chapter review, self-assessment, teacher note, writing space | chapter close |

## 3. MYP pedagogy actually carried out

* **Key and related concepts, global context and statement of inquiry** open every chapter,
  and the inquiry questions are written as factual / conceptual / debatable.
* **Four criteria with strands** are named at every section head (A knowing and
  understanding, B investigating patterns, C communicating, D applying mathematics) and
  mapped to evidence in the chapter planner table.
* **ATL skills** appear twice per chapter — once in the planner, once in the chapter body —
  in the IB's own wording, naming the skill cluster (thinking, communication,
  self-management, research, social).
* **Criterion-mapped mini-tasks** close every chapter with a criteria panel that says which
  strands the task develops and what evidence earns the mark.
* **Self-assessment** is a four-column "I can …" checklist (Not yet / Getting there /
  Confident) followed by a criteria panel, an ATL prompt, a "Looking ahead" line and a
  teacher note with ruled writing space.
* **Investigations** are open-ended and pattern-driven, not disguised exercises: each one
  asks for a conjecture, a systematic count, a generalisation and a test.
* **Real-life applications** use a single sustained context per chapter (market stall,
  storeroom, fairness relay, scaling kitchen, community market, courtyard pond, roof-truss
  workshop, clinic waiting list, courtyard repair, school travel survey).
* **Practice questions are graded** S (support) / C (core) / E (extension) by a letter tag,
  with a strand tag F (fluency), R (reasoning), P (problem solving), E (extension) on each
  item, and the practice headers state the support / core / extension range.

## 4. What was improved, relative to the source edition

1. **One display face everywhere.** The source silently swapped heading faces part-way
   through; this edition uses Playfair Display for every display setting, so the book reads
   as one volume.
2. **One baseline grid.** All body text sits on a 13.5 pt baseline; the source drifted
   between sections.
3. **A real outer margin.** Definitions, worked-example annotations, "why it matters" notes
   and ATL prompts live in a 156 pt margin column, so the reading column stays a constant
   318 pt. The margin is also the student's writing space, and the book says so.
4. **A calmer accent system.** Gold hairlines and numerals, terracotta folios and inquiry
   bars, sage for key ideas, one slate accent for data. Colour is never used to carry
   meaning on its own.
5. **Consistent running heads and folios**, with the part name at the outer edge and the
   chapter name at the inner edge, on every page.
6. **Every figure numbered and captioned**, every table numbered, and both referenced in
   the text — the source used figures decoratively.
7. **Answers to everything**, in the same order as the questions, with a note at the front
   of the answer section on what a "solution" is expected to contain.
8. **A working back matter**: 40-formula bank grouped by part, 84-entry glossary,
   114-entry index keyed to printed page numbers, and a references and acknowledgements
   section that records provenance and licences honestly.

## 5. Editorial decisions that were deliberately made

* **MYP1 only.** Nothing from Years 2–5 was pulled forward, and no Year 2 content was
  compressed to fill a gap.
* **No invented publication data.** The book carries an edition label ("Improved First
  Edition") and the author's name. It carries no ISBN, no imprint, no publisher, no print
  run and no claim of IB endorsement. The closing page states plainly that the book is an
  independent educational publication, not published by, sponsored by or affiliated with
  the International Baccalaureate Organization.
* **No copied Oxford material.** The source series was consulted for pedagogical
  inspiration only — the inquiry sequence, the balance of representations, the emphasis on
  conceptual framing. Every word and every exercise in this edition is new.
* **Illustrations are drawn, not photographed.** The ten chapter plates are original
  artwork in one flat-ink palette; there are no stock photographs. See
  `image_references/IMAGE_REGISTER.md`.

## 6. Known limitations (stated, not hidden)

1. The typeset text is produced from Python source, so a few typographic refinements that a
   page-layout program would make automatically (optical kerning across a line break,
   widow control finer than "keep with next") are handled by hand.
2. Answers for open investigations and mini-tasks give the expected result and the idea
   that earns the mark; they do not reproduce a full student response, by design.
3. The index is selective (114 terms) rather than exhaustive; it indexes concepts, not
   every occurrence of every word.
4. The book is a student edition only. A teacher edition with scheme-of-work mapping is not
   part of this deliverable.
5. Diagrams are schematic, drawn to make the mathematics visible; they are not
   to-scale technical drawings, and the text says so where scale could mislead.
