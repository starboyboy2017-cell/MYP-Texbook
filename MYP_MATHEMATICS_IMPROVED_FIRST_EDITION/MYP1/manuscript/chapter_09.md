Statement of inquiry Measurement converts shapes into numbers, and the relationship between a shape and its dimensions determines which quantity is being described.
Key and related concepts
Key concept · Relationships. Each formula relates dimensions to a measured quantity; changing a dimension
changes the quantity in a predictable way.
Related · Quantity. Length, area and volume are different kinds of quantity, not different sizes of the same one.
Related · Space. Area and volume describe how much space a figure occupies.
Global context · Orientation in space and time
Exploration: how a builder estimates material — paint by area, edging by perimeter, concrete by volume — and
what happens when the three are confused.
Inquiry questions
– Factual: What are the units of length, area and volume, and how do they relate?
– Conceptual: Why does doubling a length multiply the area by four?
– Debatable: Should a price be quoted per unit length, per unit area or per unit volume?

The distinction that matters
Perimeter is measured in units of length. Area is measured in square
units. Volume is measured in cubic units. The unit is not decoration; it is
how you know which question you answered.

9.4 Surface area and volume of prisms
Surface area and volume
The surface area of a solid is the total area of all its faces, in square units.
The volume of a prism is the area of its cross-section multiplied by its

The result
Among all rectangles with a given perimeter, the square has the largest area. This is why a fixed length of fencing
encloses most ground when the enclosure is square — and why natural cells and bubbles tend towards round
shapes, which do the same thing without corners.

What this chapter established
– Perimeter is a length; circumference is the perimeter of a circle.
– Area is measured in square units, and each formula can be derived by rearranging a rectangle.
– The area of a circle is πr2, so it grows with the square of the radius.
– Volume of a prism is cross-section area times length.
– Doubling every dimension multiplies area by 4 and volume by 8.
Vocabulary
Term Meaning
perimeter Total length of a boundary.
area Surface enclosed, in square units.
volume Space occupied, in cubic units.
cross-section The shape you see when a solid is cut straight through.
prism A solid with the same cross-section all along its length.
surface area Total area of all the faces of a solid.
capacity The volume a container can hold, often in litres.
Misconceptions to watch
Three that persist
– Bigger perimeter means bigger area. A long thin rectangle can have a large perimeter and a small area.
– Area and perimeter share units. One is in cm, the other in cm2.
– 1 m2 = 100 cm2. It is 10 000 cm2, because both dimensions convert.
