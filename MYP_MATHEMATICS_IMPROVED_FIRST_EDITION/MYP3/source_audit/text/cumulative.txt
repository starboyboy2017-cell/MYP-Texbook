Self-assessment and examination technique
Marks are lost more often to presentation than to knowledge. Before you mark your own
work, read the three habits below; then record, paper by paper, where the evidence of
your understanding actually sits.
READ THE COMMAND WORD
“Show”, “explain”, “justify” and
“evaluate” ask for different amounts
of writing. Answer the verb you
were given.
MAKE THE METHOD VISIBLE
Write the formula, substitute, then
calculate. A bare answer cannot earn
method marks, even when it is
correct.
CHECK FOR SENSE
Ask whether the size, sign and unit
of the result are plausible. A
negative length or a probability
above 1 signals an error to find.
