# Image register — MYP Mathematics 3, Improved First Edition

into the PDF as vector paths) or an **original photograph made for this edition**.
No stock photograph, web image, publisher image, scan or screenshot appears
anywhere in the book, the DOCX or this folder.

## 3. Plate artwork

The cover, the frontispiece, the four part plates, the ten chapter plates, the
reference plates and the back cover are drawn in code (`build/bookkit/plates.py`):
navy field, construction grid, gold rule, outline numeral, and one mathematical
motif per plate (surd construction, graph, solid, symmetry, scatter, tree).
They contain no third-party artwork.
